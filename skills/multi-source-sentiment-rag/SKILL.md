# Multi-Source Sentiment RAG

## Overview
This skill queries news feeds, search engines, and Chroma vector memory to synthesize ground-truth evidence bearing on market outcomes.

## Capabilities
- Ingests and summarizes real-time news articles relating to active market resolution criteria.
- Extracts statistical polling trends and assesses polling methodology reliability.
- Quantifies narrative momentum and sentiment shifts affecting market participants.
